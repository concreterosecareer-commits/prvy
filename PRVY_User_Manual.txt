Entertainers. Patrons discover performers, send tips via Gems, and
book real-world venue experiences. Entertainers manage their
profile, track earnings, and grow their audience.

1. GETTING STARTED
--------------------------------------------------------------

1.1  Creating an Account
--------------------------
1. Open the PRIVY app and tap "Get Started" or "Sign Up."
2. Enter your email address and choose a secure password.
3. Select your account type:
     - Patron       — you want to discover and connect with
                      entertainers.
     - Entertainer  — you are a performer who wants to earn
                      on the platform.
4. Tap "Create Account."
5. PRIVY will send a verification link to your email address —
   see Section 1.2.

1.2  Verifying Your Email
---------------------------
1. After signing up, you are taken to the "Check your email"
   screen.
2. Open your inbox and look for an email from PRIVY.
3. Click the "Verify my account" link inside the email.
4. You will be taken directly to your dashboard — no code
   to type.

   TIP: The link expires after 24 hours. If you do not see
   the email, check your spam folder or tap "Resend
   verification link" on the verification screen.

1.3  Logging In
-----------------
1. Open the PRIVY app and tap "Sign In."
2. Enter your registered email and password.
3. Tap "Sign In." You will be taken to your dashboard.

1.4  Resetting Your Password
------------------------------
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?"
2. Enter your email address and tap "Send Reset Link."
3. Check your inbox and follow the link to set a new password.

--------------------------------------------------------------
2. DASHBOARD OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------

The Dashboard is your home screen after logging in. It shows:

  - Welcome message with your name and role.
  - Gem Balance — your current in-app currency balance and
    its USD equivalent.
  - Unread Messages — count of new messages waiting for you.
  - Active Connections — number of people you are connected
    with.
  - Hot Spots — quick view of top venues near you.
  - Recent Activity — latest interactions on your account.

Patrons see a "Buy Gems" shortcut button in the Gem Balance
card. Entertainers see a "Check Income" shortcut instead.

--------------------------------------------------------------
3. DANCERS
--------------------------------------------------------------

3.1  Browsing Entertainers (Patrons)
---------------------------------
1. Tap "Entertainers" in the left sidebar.
2. Browse performer cards showing their name, username,
   location, and a short bio.
3. Use the search bar at the top to find a entertainer by name
   or username.
4. Filter results by city or category using the filter chips.
5. Tap a entertainer's card to view their full profile, then:
     - Tap "Message" to start a conversation.
     - Tap the gift icon to send a Gem tip.

3.2  Managing Your Profile (Entertainers)
--------------------------------------
1. Tap "Account" or your avatar in the sidebar to open your
   profile.
2. Update your display name, username, bio, location, and
   profile photo.
3. Your profile is visible to all Patrons on the platform.

--------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOT SPOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Hot Spots are real-world venues where Entertainers and Patrons
meet and connect.

1. Tap "Hot Spots" in the sidebar.
2. Browse venues grouped by city.
3. Use the city filter tabs at the top to narrow results to
   a specific city.
4. Tap a venue card to see its full details — address,
   hours, and which entertainers are associated with it.
5. Tap "Visit" or the link on the venue card for directions.

--------------------------------------------------------------
5. MESSAGES & CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

5.1  Connections
------------------
1. Tap "Connections" in the sidebar.
2. You will see three tabs:
     - Connected    — people you are already connected with.
     - Invitations  — pending connection requests you have
                      received.
     - Suggested    — people you may want to connect with.
3. To accept an invitation, tap the green check button.
4. To decline, tap the red X button.
5. To connect with someone from Suggested, tap "Connect."

5.2  Messages
---------------
1. Tap "Messages" in the sidebar.
2. Your conversation list appears on the left (or top on
   mobile).
3. Tap a conversation to open it.
4. Type your message in the text box at the bottom and press
   Enter or tap the send button.
5. You can send Gem tips directly from the message thread.

--------------------------------------------------------------
6. GEMS & WALLET
--------------------------------------------------------------

6.1  What Are Gems?
---------------------
Gems are PRIVY's in-app currency. Patrons buy Gems and use
them to tip Entertainers, unlock premium content, and send gifts.
Entertainers receive Gems as earnings and can withdraw them as USD.

  100 Gems = $1.00 USD (default rate — may vary)

6.2  Buying Gems
------------------
1. Tap "Buy Gems" in the sidebar, or tap "Buy Gems" from
   your Wallet or Dashboard.
2. Select a Gem package:
     - 500 Gems    — $10
     - 1,000 Gems  — $20
     - 2,500 Gems  — $50
     - 5,000 Gems  — $100
     - 10,000 Gems — $200  (Best Value)
     - Custom amount — enter a number manually.
3. Choose a payment method:
     - Coinbase Pay
     - MetaMask
     - Wallet Connect
4. Tap "Complete Purchase" to finalize.

6.3  Your Wallet
------------------
1. Tap "Wallet" in the sidebar.
2. Your wallet card shows your current Gem balance and its
   USD value.
3. VIP members see a "VIP" badge on their wallet card.
4. Quick-buy buttons (500 / 1K / 2.5K / 5K / 10K) let you
   top up fast without going to the full Buy Gems page.
5. Entertainers also see a withdrawal section to cash out their
   Gem earnings to USD.

6.4  Transactions
-------------------
1. Tap "Transactions" in the sidebar.
2. See a full history of your Gem purchases, tips sent,
   tips received, and withdrawals.
3. Each entry shows the date, amount, type, and status
   (Completed / Pending / Failed).

--------------------------------------------------------------
7. ANALYTICS  (Entertainers Only)
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. Tap "Analytics" in the sidebar.
2. View your earnings performance over time:
     - Earnings chart — daily/weekly revenue trend.
     - Earnings by source — breakdown (tips, subscriptions,
       etc.).
     - Total Gems received, total USD earned, and active
       patron count.
3. Use the date range selector to filter by period.

--------------------------------------------------------------
8. INVITE & EARN
--------------------------------------------------------------

PRIVY rewards you for growing the community.

1. Tap "Invite & Earn" in the sidebar.
2. Copy your unique invite link and share it with friends.
3. When someone signs up using your link and becomes a
   Entertainer, you earn 15% of their Gem earnings automatically.
4. Your invite earnings appear in your Wallet and
   Transactions history.
5. The Invite & Earn page also shows a list of people you
   have already invited and their current status.

--------------------------------------------------------------
9. NOTIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

1. Tap the bell icon (top bar) or "Notifications" in the
   sidebar.
2. See alerts for:
     - New connection requests.
     - Messages received.
     - Gem tips received.
     - Platform announcements.
3. Tap any notification to go directly to the relevant
   screen.

--------------------------------------------------------------

Account
--------
1. Tap "Account" in the sidebar.
2. Edit your display name, username, bio, and profile photo.
3. Tap "Save" to apply changes.

Settings

12. ADMIN PANEL  (Staff Only)
--------------------------------------------------------------

The Admin Panel is a separate area for PRIVY staff to manage
the platform. It is not accessible to regular users.

12.1  Logging In
------------------
1. Navigate to: yourdomain.com/admin/login
2. Enter the admin email and password.
   Default credentials (change after first login):
     Email:    admin@PRIVY.com
     Password: admin2024
3. Click "Sign In." You are taken to the Admin Dashboard.

   NOTE: Admin credentials are controlled by the ADMIN_EMAIL
   and ADMIN_PASSWORD environment variables on the server.
   Update these in your hosting platform (e.g., Vercel) for
   production use.

12.2  Admin Dashboard
-----------------------
The dashboard gives a live platform overview:
  - Total Users, Entertainers, Patrons, Revenue, Gems Sold,
    Active Now — displayed as stat cards.
  - User Management table — quick view of the 6 most recent
    users with inline action buttons.
  - Recent Signups — newest members.
  - Platform Health — today's messages, connections, and
    active Hot Spots.

12.3  User Management
-----------------------
1. Click "Users" in the admin sidebar.
2. See the full list of all platform users.
3. Use the filter chips to view:
     - All / Entertainers / Patrons / Suspended users.
4. Use the search bar to find a user by name or username.
5. Each row has three action buttons:
     - Eye icon     — View the user's full profile (opens a
                      side panel with email, location, gems,
                      sessions, and join date).
     - Shield icon  — Suspend or Unsuspend the user.
                      (Amber = suspend active user;
                       Green  = unsuspend suspended user.)
     - Ban icon     — Ban or Unban the user.
                      (Red   = ban active user;
                       Green = unban banned user.)
6. Status changes reflect immediately in the table and
   status badge.
7. The "..." (More) menu on each row also provides:
     - Suspend / Unsuspend
     - Delete user (removes them from the table permanently).

12.4  Transactions
--------------------
1. Click "Transactions" in the admin sidebar.
2. View all platform financial activity:
     - Total Revenue, Total Inflow, Total Outflow — summary
       cards at the top.
     - Full transaction table with user, type, amount, date,
       and status columns.

12.5  Reports
---------------
1. Click "Reports" in the admin sidebar.
2. See all user-submitted reports at a glance:
     - Summary cards: Total / Open / Resolved counts.
     - Full reports table with reporter, target, reason,
       date, and status.
3. Click "Review" on an open report to investigate it.
   Resolved reports show a "Done" badge and the button is
   disabled.

12.6  Admin Settings
----------------------
1. Click "Settings" in the admin sidebar.
2. Manage platform-wide configuration:

   Financial Settings
     - Default Commission Rate (%) applied to entertainer payouts.
     - Gem-to-USD conversion rate.
     - Minimum withdrawal amount ($).

   Platform Controls (toggle switches)
     - New Signups     — allow/block new registrations.
     - Email Verification — require OTP before account access.
     - Two-Factor Auth — enforce 2FA for admin accounts.
     - Maintenance Mode — temporarily disable patron access.
     - Explicit Content — allow/block adult media uploads.

   Admin Notifications
     - New user signup
     - New report filed
     - Large withdrawal (> $500)
     - Failed payment

3. Click "Save Changes" to apply, or "Discard" to cancel.

TIPS & BEST PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------

  - Keep your profile photo up to date so patrons can
    recognise you.
  - Entertainers: check Analytics weekly to understand which
    content drives the most Gem income.
  - Patrons: the 10,000 Gem package offers the best value
    per dollar.
  - Use the Invite & Earn programme — 15% passive income
    adds up quickly.
  - If you believe a user is violating community guidelines,
    use the report button on their profile.
